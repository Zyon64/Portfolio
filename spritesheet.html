Z · game sprite sheet

The game can use your own art. In the game open Pause → Options: DOWNLOAD SHEET gives you the default art, DOWNLOAD GUIDE gives the same image with a grid, names and the safe drawing area of every piece (do not load the guide), and LOAD SHEET loads your edited PNG. USE DEFAULT goes back to the built-in art. Your sheet is remembered in this browser.

How it works

  • The sheet is pixels: a grid of cells. One piece per cell.
  • The centre of each cell is the pivot: the enemy's centre, or the point a part rotates and orbits around.
  • Animated parts are separate cells (green border below). The game places, rotates, scales and animates them exactly as before; you draw each one once, pointing right (+x).
  • Bodies are scaled by the real enemy size, so big, small and juggernaut enemies reuse the same drawing and nothing is cropped.
  • Each piece only shows its safe area: a square around the centre. The sheet already has guide lines for it, drawn just outside that area so the game never reads them: a green frame = how far the sprite is visible (anything beyond is cut), yellow ticks = the axes through the pivot, a grey frame = the cell edge (64×64). Draw inside the green frame.
  • Pieces marked tinted are colour-coded by the game (behaviour, frozen, hit-flash): only their shape (alpha) is used.
  • Pixels are never smoothed. Keep the PNG at the exact size above.
