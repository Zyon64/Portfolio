Z · game sprite sheet

The game can use your own art. In the game open Pause → Options: DOWNLOAD SHEET gives you the default art, DOWNLOAD GUIDE gives the same image with each piece's name written on it (do not load the guide), and LOAD SHEET loads your edited PNG. USE DEFAULT goes back to the built-in art. Your sheet is remembered in this browser.

How it works

  • The sheet is pixels: cells, columns × rows.
  • One row per sprite type. The first cell of the row is the sprite. The cells to its right are frames: if you draw frames 2, 3, 4… (without leaving gaps) that piece becomes an animation and the game cycles through them.
  • If the first cell of a row is empty, the game ignores your sheet for that piece and uses the default art, so nothing breaks.
  • The centre of each cell is the pivot: the enemy's centre, or the point a part rotates and orbits around.
  • Animated parts are their own rows (marked “layer”). The game places, rotates, scales and animates them; you draw each one once, pointing right (+x).
  • Bodies are scaled by the real enemy size, so big, small and juggernaut enemies reuse the same drawing.
  • Each piece only shows its safe area, a square around the centre. The sheet has guide lines drawn just outside it so the game never reads them: green frame = how far the sprite is visible (anything beyond is cut), yellow ticks = the axes through the pivot, grey frame = the cell edge. All 8 frame cells of every row have guides.
  • Collisions follow your drawing. The hit area of a body is the silhouette of its pixels (all frames, filled in, hollow outlines count as solid). Your own shape also decides where you can be hit.
  • Weapon icons (rows at the bottom) appear when you choose a weapon and in the weapon stat. They can be animated like any other row.
  • Pieces marked tinted are colour-coded by the game (behaviour, frozen, hit-flash): only their shape (alpha) is used.
  • Pixels are never smoothed. Keep the PNG at the exact size above.
